## Pricing
- Both plans include the same complete custom website: unlimited pages, brand and logo design, accessibility pages, mobile-ready pages, SEO, forms and click-to-call, reviews, and a review before launch
- Every website is a one-time $199 buildout, then the client chooses Care or Hosting
- Care: $49/mo all-inclusive, with hosting, unlimited edits and pages, support, lead form emails sent to the client, and 1 blog post included. Extra blog posts are $25
- Hosting: $99/yr, billed yearly only (no monthly option), covering hosting and maintenance. Edits are $50 per round, up to 2 pages. Blog posts are $25 each. The client is responsible for their own form email receiving service
- After the second month of Care, clients can switch to Hosting only and are not charged for hosting for that first year
- Clients who cancel and want to keep the website code pay a one-time $199, regardless of prior payments
